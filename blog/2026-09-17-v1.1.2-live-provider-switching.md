# SwarmAI 1.1.2: Live Provider Switching & In-Picker Model Search

We are excited to ship **SwarmAI v1.1.2**, introducing two of the most requested features by developers working with diverse LLM workflows: **Live Provider Switching** and **In-Picker Search**.

---

## 🔄 Live Provider Switching

Previously, selecting a model lock-in occurred at the beginning of a chat thread. If you started with Claude 3.7 Sonnet for brainstorming and later wanted to invoke Codex or Gemini 2.5 Pro for deep implementation, you had to spin up a separate thread.

With **v1.1.2**, you can seamlessly switch providers and models in an ongoing conversation.
- The full conversation history, past tool outputs, and worktree state are preserved.
- The newly selected provider picks up right where the previous model left off.
- You can leverage the unique strengths of different models at each stage of a complex engineering turn.

---

## 🔍 Instant In-Picker Search

With over 20+ models and Hydra configurations across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, Meta, and Copilot, scrolling to find a specific model could be slow.

The new model picker now features an instant, reactive search filter:
- Type to instantly find models, providers, and Hydra pairs.
- Real-time display of token quotas, reasoning effort compatibility, and context lengths.
- Keyboard navigation friendly.
